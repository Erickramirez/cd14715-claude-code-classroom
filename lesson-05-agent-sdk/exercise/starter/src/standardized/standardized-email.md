### Risk Assessment

**⚠️ CRITICAL ISSUES - HIGH RISK CONTRACT:**

1. **Not a Formal Contract** - This is an informal email proposal, not a legally binding contract
2. **Missing Customer Identity** - Customer party not clearly identified (just "Team")
3. **No Effective Date** - Start date not specified
4. **No Scope Definition** - "Website redesign services" lacks specific deliverables, specifications, or requirements
5. **No Completion Criteria** - No definition of what constitutes "completion" for final payment
6. **No IP Rights Assignment** - Ownership of design work and code not addressed
7. **No Liability Protections** - No limitation of liability, indemnification, or warranty terms
8. **No Termination Clause** - No provisions for early termination or cancellation
9. **No Dispute Resolution** - No mechanism for resolving disagreements
10. **No Confidentiality Terms** - No protection for sensitive business information
11. **No Change Order Process** - No procedure for scope changes or additional work
12. **No Late Payment Terms** - No penalties or interest for delayed payments
13. **No Force Majeure** - No provisions for unforeseen circumstances
14. **Informal Communication** - Casual tone inappropriate for legal document
15. **Missing Legal Requirements** - No governing law, venue, or jurisdiction clauses

**RECOMMENDATION:** This informal email should NOT be used as a contract. A formal written agreement should be drafted that includes all missing terms before any work begins or deposits are paid.
